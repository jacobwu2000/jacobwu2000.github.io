## Ancient Testimonia

[PLACEHOLDER: literary references, including the mention of the Asclepieion in Pausanias.]

<PersonalObservations>
The less-popular archaeological site at Argos consisted of the Agora, the Baths, the Odeon, and the Theater. Next to the surprisingly large Hellenistic theater was the Serapeion-Asclepeion, separated by a mudbrick wall. During the reign of Pharaoh Ptolemy I Soter, many Egyptian gods were brought over and syncretized with Greek ones, resulting in the syncretic deity Serapis, who was closely linked with Asclepius as a healing god who appeared to people in their dreams. In the times of Hadrian, what had originally been the worship of Serapis was replaced by an Asclepieion, which is mentioned in Pausanias. In Roman times, the sanctuary was converted into a public bath complex.
